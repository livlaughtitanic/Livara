LEGAL & PRIVACY

Your privacy matters.

This policy explains what information Livara collects, why it is used and the choices you have when you visit this website or get in touch.

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Livara is committed to handling personal information responsibly and being clear about how it is used. This policy applies to visitors to livara.ie and people who contact Livara about its services.

Who we are

For the purposes of data protection law, Livara, operated by Liv Roche in Ireland, is the data controller for the personal information described in this policy. This means Livara decides why and how that information is used.

You can contact Livara about this policy or your personal information at liv@livara.ie.

Information we collect

Information you provide

When you submit the contact form or email Livara, you may provide:

  • Your name and email address.
  • Your business name and website address.
  • The service you are interested in.
  • The details and any other information you choose to include in your message.

Information collected when you visit the website

Depending on your cookie and tracking choices, technical and usage information may be collected when you browse the website. This may include your device and browser information, approximate location derived from your IP address, pages viewed, interactions, referral source and online identifiers such as cookie IDs.

How we use your information

Livara uses personal information where it is necessary to:

  • Respond to enquiries and communicate with you.
  • Discuss, prepare and provide proposals or services you request.
  • Deliver agreed work and maintain relevant business records.
  • Understand how visitors use the website and improve its content and performance, where the required consent has been given.
  • Maintain website security, prevent misuse and troubleshoot technical issues.
  • Meet applicable legal, accounting and regulatory obligations.

Why we are allowed to use your information

Data protection law requires a lawful basis for using personal information. Depending on the circumstances, Livara relies on:

  • Steps before entering a contract or performing a contract: to respond when you ask about services, prepare a proposal or provide work you have agreed to.
  • Legitimate interests: to manage business correspondence, maintain appropriate records, protect and secure the website and operate the business, where those interests are not overridden by your rights.
  • Legal obligation: where information must be retained or provided to comply with applicable law.
  • Consent: for non-essential cookies and similar tracking technologies, including analytics where consent is required. You can change or withdraw your consent using the website's cookie settings.

Who processes information on our behalf

Livara uses a small number of service providers to operate the website and communicate with you. Depending on the service involved, these may include:

  • Formspree: processes information submitted through the website contact form and passes the enquiry to Livara.
  • Google Workspace: is used for business email and may process messages and contact details when you communicate with Livara.
  • Google Analytics 4: provides website usage statistics and performance insights where the relevant tracking is enabled and consent has been obtained where required.
  • Ketch: provides the website's consent-management functionality, allowing visitors to manage cookie and tracking preferences.
  • Website hosting and infrastructure providers: deliver the website and may process technical information, such as server logs and IP addresses, to provide and secure the service.

These providers process information under their own privacy terms and, where applicable, contractual data-processing arrangements. Livara does not sell personal information or share it with third parties for their own direct marketing.

Information may also be disclosed where necessary to comply with a legal obligation, respond to a lawful request or establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

Cookies and website analytics

The website uses Ketch to manage cookie and tracking preferences. It may use Google Analytics 4 to understand general website traffic and how visitors use the site.

Non-essential analytics cookies and similar tracking technologies should only be used where you have given the required consent. You can accept, reject or adjust your preferences through the cookie controls made available on the website. You can also withdraw or change your choice later through those controls.

For more information about the technologies used, their purposes and how to manage your choices, please read our Cookie Policy.

Your browser may also offer settings to block or delete cookies. Blocking some cookies may affect how certain website features work.

How long we keep information

Livara keeps personal information only for as long as it is reasonably needed for the purpose for which it was collected, including to respond to enquiries, provide services, maintain business records and meet legal obligations.

When information is no longer needed, it will be securely deleted or anonymised where appropriate. The period may vary depending on the type of information, whether you become a client and any legal record-keeping requirements that apply.

Where information is processed

Some of the service providers used by Livara may process or store information outside the European Economic Area (EEA). Where personal information is transferred internationally, the provider and Livara must rely on an appropriate transfer mechanism or safeguard required by data protection law.

Your data protection rights

Under the GDPR, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal information Livara holds about you.
  • Ask for inaccurate or incomplete information to be corrected.
  • Request deletion of your information in certain circumstances.
  • Ask for the use of your information to be restricted in certain circumstances.
  • Object to certain types of processing.
  • Request a copy of information you provided in a commonly used, machine-readable format where the right to data portability applies.
  • Withdraw consent at any time where processing is based on consent. This does not affect processing that took place before consent was withdrawn.

These rights are subject to conditions and exemptions under data protection law. To make a request, email liv@livara.ie. Livara may need to verify your identity before responding.

You also have the right to make a complaint to the Irish Data Protection Commission. Information is available at dataprotection.ie.

How we protect information

Livara takes reasonable technical and organisational steps to protect personal information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse or disclosure. However, no method of transmitting or storing information online can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Links to other websites

This website may contain links to external websites. Livara is not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those websites. You should review their privacy information before providing them with personal information.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to the website, the services Livara uses or applicable legal requirements. The latest version will be published on this page with its updated date.

QUESTIONS ABOUT YOUR DATA?

Get in touch.

If you have a question about this policy or want to exercise your data protection rights, contact Livara at liv@livara.ie.
