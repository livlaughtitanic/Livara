How I work

Practical advice, shaped around your business.

Liv Roche
Founder of Livara

Based in Ireland, working remotely.

Hi, I’m Liv. I started Livara to help businesses make clearer decisions about their digital activity, without making the process more complicated than it needs to be.

Every business is at a different stage. Priorities change, customers change, and what worked last year might not be what the business needs now. I don’t think there’s much value in recommending the same solution to everyone.

Instead, I like to understand what you’re trying to achieve, look at the information available, and work out where your time and budget are likely to be best spent. Sometimes that means improving something you already have. Sometimes it means taking a step back and figuring out what is missing.

You can expect clear explanations, honest recommendations and practical next steps. I want you to understand the reasoning behind the work, not feel like you have to take my word for it.

What matters to me

A thoughtful approach, without the fuss.

Good work should fit the business you have today, while leaving room for where you want to take it next.

01 / Start with the facts

Understand before changing

I look at what is already in place and what the available data can tell us before suggesting changes. That gives us a more useful starting point than guesswork.

02 / Keep it practical

Make the next step clear

Recommendations should be understandable and realistic to put into practice. More tools or more activity are not automatically the answer.

03 / Leave room to grow

Adapt as things change

Businesses develop, and plans should be able to develop with them. I focus on what makes sense for your current priorities, not a one-size-fits-all formula.
